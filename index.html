Mystery Colouring Studio

Free • Works on iPhone • Images stay on your device

1. Choose an illustration

Choose a clear illustration with a distinct animal and contrasting background. No account or upload to a server.

Select an image to begin.

2. Review the actual grid

Fix colours and squares

To fix a colour everywhere, tap its colour box below and choose a replacement. To fix only certain squares, choose the correct colour and tap the wrong squares on the completed solution. Both exports will update.

Tip: correcting a palette colour changes every square with that number. Painting changes only the squares you tap. Changing image settings and regenerating clears square edits.

3. Save your files

On iPhone, use Print / Save PDF and save through the Share menu. PNG exports can also be sent for book compilation.
